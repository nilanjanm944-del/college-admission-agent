Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GRE mandatory for all programs? ▶
GRE is optional for Indian applicants but mandatory for international applicants. A score of 300+ is recommended for a competitive application.
Can I apply to multiple programs? ▶
Yes, you may apply to up to 2 programs. A separate application fee is required for each program.
When will I receive the admission decision? ▶
Merit lists are declared on 10 May 2025. Shortlisted candidates will be notified via email and SMS within 48 hours of the list being published.
What documents do I need to submit at verification? ▶
You need: original degree certificates, mark sheets, government ID proof, passport-size photographs, category certificate (if applicable), and test score reports.
Is there a scholarship available? ▶
Yes. Merit scholarships are awarded to the top 10% of admitted students in each program. Need-based financial aid is also available — apply separately after receiving admission.
How does the AI admission predictor work? ▶
The predictor uses a machine learning model trained on historical admissions data, powered by IBM watsonx.ai. It takes your GRE, TOEFL, CGPA, SOP/LOR strength, university rating, and research experience to estimate your probability of admission. It is indicative only and does not guarantee admission.
